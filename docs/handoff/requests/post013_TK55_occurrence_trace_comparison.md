### Invalid-present capability correction after independent audit

The initial prototype incorrectly caught malformed range unpacking/comparison as `ABSTAIN_DECODER`. Validation now precedes and is separate from the decoder exception boundary. Source/rendered are exact strings; selections and origins are immutable exact tuples. Each range is exactly two integer endpoints (bool/float/nonfinite excluded), nonnegative, ordered, nonoverlapping and within the snapshot. An empty selection has an explicit zero-width range. Owned origins are exact integer indices, unique/monotone, within selection, and match the rendered characters; inserted characters alone may use `None`. The trace's rendered leaf must equal the actual declared consumer leaf. An absent opaque trace is distinguished from malformed present data; even opaque data cannot hide invalid source/selection metadata. These checks validate factory output, not authenticate arbitrary caller-supplied tuples as a consumer capability.
