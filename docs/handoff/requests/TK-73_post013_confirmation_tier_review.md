# TK73 concrete confirmation-tier review — approval required

This proposes a review contract, not a product change. User approval covers reviewing the proposal only. Existing findings/status/grades, tests936, scorer, baselines and acceptance requirements remain unchanged. The bounded108 equality scope is not the proposed solution.

**Keep A.** Independently source-proved complete TITLE differences retain A. Preserve the original GLOBAL population and complete alternatives across every parent/party/number/branch: competing inventory captions, competing full propositions, declared title/qualification/date differences and uncertain origins cannot disappear through a local comparison. Existing TK64 populations, calendar/gap/amount/list-missing findings and old source/protection guards remain. Distinct plain names, protected literals, recognized-list populations and all existing GLOBAL vetoes keep the legacy A fallback even without a new formal-title certificate. “Legacy A retained” must be distinguished from “independent TITLE conflict proved” in the review receipt.

**Bounded pending candidate.** Consider an existing duplicate only when the whole same-key group is original-source complete, belongs to one independently proved parent, contains one source-certified scoped-inventory complete value and one outside-inventory colon-shaped complete value, and no formal-TITLE or original GLOBAL/protection veto applies. Inventory proof requires original heading/entry/close occurrences and at least two independent reference-colon entries; no interval is extended. A colon-shaped payload is a formatting observation, not a predicate/NON_TITLE certificate. Its complete text stays intact. Do not require or infer equality, discard a remainder, join weak evidence, or exempt a registered key. Missing/ambiguous source, mixed keys/parents, repeated conflicting complete alternatives, dates/protected context, other shapes or failed checks retain original A. Generic UNKNOWN alone never activates pending.

**Meaning and limitation.** Pending says “the two original inscriptions differ; whether they are different formal titles needs confirmation.” It does not assert equal titles or absence of duplication. Genuine unproved mixed-format titles can also enter this tier. That is a confirmation loss, even when the original finding and A-grade source evidence remain in the audit record. Public same-title/different-description and genuine-different-title counterparts must show this tradeoff. Detection retention cannot be described as preserved confirmation recall.

**Report four quantities separately.** Keep original A findings, independently confirmed TITLE-A, legacy-A retained and pending proposals with their original source/key/complete payloads. Report true duplicates moved to pending, detected-true totals and confirmed-true totals separately, plus pending false candidates. The unchanged original AFP measure still counts the original pending A findings. A confirmation-only FP measure is a different proposed metric and cannot be called original AFP0 or replace total/control FP. No error-counter shifting, silent removal or detection/confirmation conflation.

**Exact approval conflicts.** (U) Original role-UNKNOWN fallback currently asserts an A/CONTRADICTED formal-title defect; using a user-facing pending status instead changes that assertion contract. (P) Existing936 genuine mixed-format/UNKNOWN A expectations may retain detection/grade but lose confirmation; those affected cases must be listed before any status/display/test change. (S) Existing same-title controls expecting no canonical duplicate must remain unchanged wherever the old comparison already resolves them; this proposal only triages existing warnings. (M) Original AFP0/recall gates do not become satisfied by pending. Altering status semantics, confirmation coverage requirements, any expectation or metric requires separate explicit user approval and evaluator-owned changes. GLOBAL, source, TK64/date and certified-title protections are not proposed for relaxation.

First deliverable is a source-only parallel public receipt against unchanged current outputs. Actual native title-field positives, untyped mixed-format true/false counterparts and source/GLOBAL/parent/quote failures must be measured. Operational row-free controls remain residuals.
