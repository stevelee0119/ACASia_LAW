# TK73 title-conflict confidence versus non-title proof

Question: do different original description strings independently justify an A-grade finding that formal exhibit names differ? No: lexical difference proves differing text, not differing formal names. But lack of a formal-title certificate also does not prove equal titles or a non-title role. Under the retained contract, source/title uncertainty remains UNKNOWN and uses the original complete comparison fallback. A general uncertainty-based exemption or grade reduction would change that contract and erase required genuine unquoted subtitle/proposition conflicts.

A separate path can rely on positive **NON_TITLE** ownership proof rather than title equality. A native purpose field is one example: its original cell boundaries, header role and exact reference ownership prove that its contents occupy a purpose field. Their difference need not establish a formal-name conflict. This is a source-role fact, not a claim that the two formal titles are equal. A title field may itself contain a complete affirmative, negative, conditional or qualified sentence; finite grammar cannot override its original TITLE ownership. Existing authoritative PDF table handling already preserves this distinction and must remain unchanged. Native DOCX ownership is independently verifiable, but the current DOCX consumer is row-free for these tables; it is an existing coverage residual, not a passing operational title control.

The public bidirectional capability copies identical line payloads from two differently owned native tables. In one table, the differing clauses occupy purpose cells and the same complete title is retained; in the other, those entire clauses are part of distinct formal title cells. Actual PDF/DOCX tables provide original structured ownership. Their identical untyped TXT/PDF/DOCX line counterparts have no equivalent role evidence and retain the legacy A-grade fallback. Native tables are controls of the proof distinction, not a new table patch or an explanation of the unresolved line-row target. No role follows from a generic heading, balanced punctuation, finite ending, opaque string or arbitrary layout alone.

GLOBAL handling is unchanged: every original same-key alternative, unknown origin, other parent/branch and complete proposition remains in the original population; source-proved non-title content cannot conceal a competing whole formal title. Original TK64, dates, amount/list-missing, grades and all prior936 expectations remain mandatory. No new exemption key, blanket body exclusion, source sidecar or product rule is implemented. Raw source ownership is distinct from normalized reading mapping; the generated native table controls inspect original XML/cell geometry, not attrs.lines strings.

Conclusion under current proof contracts: a positive non-title certificate is independently safe in its proven source scope, while a missing title certificate alone offers no allowed suppression path. Without independently evidenced NON_TITLE ownership or a complete-caption identity relation, avoiding the original conflict would require relaxing UNKNOWN fallback, GLOBAL complete conflicts or genuine-A expectations. All are forbidden. Current diagnostics establish that limitation; they do not establish a target-applicable fix. Further implementation requires a new public source-role proof that earns both directions before product approval.
